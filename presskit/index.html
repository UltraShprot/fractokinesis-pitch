About

Bibok is a small robot boy with one trick: telekinesis. He lands on tiny planets with their own gravity, and everything around him is ammo: rocks, debris, liquids and the bodies of his enemies.

The more you fight, the less planet is left. Every boulder you hurl leaves a hole under your feet, so each arena slowly crumbles into a ring of debris. Heavy rocks hit harder, light snow bursts on impact, and elemental power-ups turn stone into ice, lava, gas or acid that mix with each other.

Each run takes you through a new route of galaxies, from a dusty first system to a forge, the snow and beyond, with a boss at the end of every galaxy.

Features

  • Destructible pixel planets with radial gravity: the ground is your weapon and your floor
  • Custom pixel physics: falling terrain and liquids that mix
  • 8 galaxies, 12 bosses and 9 mini-bosses, 130+ enemies
  • Items, power-ups and abilities that change how you throw
  • Solo or 2-player couch co-op, plus Remote Play Together
  • 31 languages, Steam achievements, gamepad support
