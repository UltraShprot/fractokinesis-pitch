About

Fractokinesis is an action roguelike on tiny planets, each with its own gravity. You play as Bibok, a small robot with telekinesis. No guns, no swords — just a whole planet under your feet.

Rip a chunk out of the ground, spin it up and send it flying into the monsters crawling your way. The heavier the rock and the faster the throw, the harder it hits. Stone, crystal, metal, bone — every material has its own weight and punch. Just remember: every chunk you tear out is ground you were standing on.

Sparks turn plain rock into a weapon. Freeze it, melt it into lava, fill it with gas and set it on fire, coat it in acid, shatter it into shards or send it into orbit. Sparks combine — gas plus fire makes an explosion — and your special ability, from eye laser to headbutt, carries every Spark you've got.

Every planet is a round arena. Enemies come from all sides, leap over you and drop from above. Lava runs down the slopes, acid eats through the ground, and no planet looks the same after a fight.

Every run builds a new galaxy. Hop from planet to planet before the darkness catches up. Find secret treasure planets, free prisoners from their cages, haggle with the merchant brothers — and fight your way to the boss.

And yes, there's Mister Hat. He'll greet you on the first planet and answer your questions — and if you're very lucky, you'll get to become Mister Hat yourself. Mister Hat only gives good advice. Probably.

Key features

  • Destructible pixel planets with radial gravity: the ground is your weapon and your floor
  • Custom pixel physics: falling terrain and liquids that mix
  • 8 galaxies, 12 bosses and 9 mini-bosses, 130+ enemies
  • Items, power-ups and abilities that change how you throw
  • Solo or 2-player couch co-op, plus Remote Play Together
  • 31 languages, Steam achievements, gamepad support
